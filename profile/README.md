## Also on the workbench

[ICERP](https://icerp.ic-labs.cloud) is our ERP for Moroccan small and medium businesses, bringing sales, purchases, inventory, documents, and finances into one workspace. It's currently in beta.

We also take on occasional client websites. [Have a look at the selected work.](https://ic-labs.cloud/work)

## Who's behind this?

[Itri Ibouanane](https://github.com/ItriIbouanane), a self-taught software engineer in Casablanca who started with JavaScript Discord bots. These days, the work includes TypeScript apps, open-source libraries, and self-managed infrastructure.

The stack depends on the project. You'll see TypeScript, React, Node.js, Bun, and tools like Convex and Coolify around here.
